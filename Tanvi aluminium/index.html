UNCOMPROMISING STANDARDS

The Tanvi Fabrication Benchmark

Why architects and structural engineers trust Tanvi Aluminium for critical high-rise and luxury projects.

Alloy 6063-T6 Extrusions

We exclusively fabricate with virgin architectural aluminium alloy 6063-T6, offering superior tensile strength, pristine anodizing response, and zero micro-cracks.

  • Minimum wall thickness 1.5mm - 2.5mm
  • High corrosion resistance in humid weather

CNC 45° Precision Mitering

Every corner is machined on heavy-duty double-head mitre saws with heavy internal corner cleats, ensuring 100% gapless seams that never sag or loosen over time.

  • Micro-gap sealant prevents water ingress
  • Concealed heavy-gauge corner brackets

Structural Silicone Sealants

We apply certified structural silicone (Dow Corning / GE Silicones) engineered for high wind loads, UV resistance, and thermal expansion across extreme temperature cycles.

  • 100% leak-proof sealing guarantee
  • High movement joint accommodation

Acoustic & Weather Gaskets

All sliding and glazing channels are lined with high-density vulcanized EPDM rubber gaskets and wool pile weather-strips to isolate traffic roar, dust, and wind whistling.

  • Noise reduction up to 38-45 dB
  • UV-resistant rubber prevents hardening
